LOCAL LISTINGS

Privacy policy · Last updated November 2, 2026

Local Listings is a TV guide for the shows and movies you follow across streaming services. This page explains what the app stores and why.

What stays on your phone

Your list of shows and movies, your streaming subscriptions and app settings are saved in your phone's browser storage. If you add a TMDB key, it is stored only on that device and never sent to us.

If you create an account

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with email or Google, we store:

  • your email address (and, with Google, your name as Google provides it),
  • the shows and movies you follow and the subscriptions you select.

We use this only to sign you in and keep your list in sync across devices. Accounts are hosted by Supabase. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Show data

Schedules come from TVmaze, and movie and where-to-watch details from TMDB (where-to-watch data by JustWatch). The app asks these services for the titles you look up; it does not send them your account details.

Deleting your data

Remove shows in the app at any time. To delete your account and everything stored with it, email the address below and we will delete it within 30 days.
